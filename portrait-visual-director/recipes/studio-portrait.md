# studio-portrait

# Intent

Controlled studio portraiture with precise background, light, separation, and pose—clean and intentional without defaulting to a commercial studio cliché.

# Visual Direction

Treat background tone, subject distance, shadow behavior, and light ratio as one system. The frame may be simple, but it must not be visually accidental.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `environment` | Seamless paper, cyclorama, or deliberately controlled studio surface | NORMAL |
| `subject.pose` | Precise but human stance with resolved hands and shoulders | NORMAL |
| `composition.framing` | Clean edge control and purposeful crop | NORMAL |
| `camera` | Perspective flattering the requested shot scale without flattening identity | SUBTLE |
| `lighting.source` | One defined studio key; fill or separation only when needed | NORMAL |
| `lighting.direction` | Consistent face, body, floor, and backdrop shadow logic | NORMAL |
| `color.palette` | Background and wardrobe separated by tone or hue | NORMAL |
| `texture.skin` | Natural skin under controlled light; restrained retouch | NORMAL |

# Director Guidance

Specify backdrop tone and subject-to-background separation. Use modifiers or rim light only to solve a visible problem. Let pose precision coexist with asymmetry, breath, and natural joints.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK and wardrobe references remain primary. Pose ADAPT may set line and weight. Lighting INSPIRE can replace the default key if it remains physically coherent with the backdrop.

# Protective Check

Face, clothing, floor contact, cast shadows, and backdrop must share one studio light model; no cutout halo or conflicting shadow directions.

# Must Not Override

Explicit backdrop color, lighting LOCK, identity, aspect, garment facts, or “不要影楼摆拍感”.

# Avoid

Poreless skin, rigid symmetry, stock hand-on-chin poses, unexplained three-rim setups, floating feet, dirty seamless edges, or generic photo-studio props.
