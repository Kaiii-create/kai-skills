# professional-portrait

# Intent

Credible professional and personal-brand portraiture that communicates role, warmth, authority, or approachability without stock-photo theater.

# Visual Direction

Translate the stated profession and audience into an appropriate degree of formality, environment, clothing, expression, and crop. Do not assume every professional wears a suit.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `subject.expression` | Credible and engaged; warmth/authority matched to role | NORMAL |
| `subject.gaze` | Direct or near-direct connection unless context requires otherwise | NORMAL |
| `subject.pose` | Open posture with resolved shoulders and hands | NORMAL |
| `wardrobe.outfit` | Role-appropriate, current, understated, well-fitted | NORMAL |
| `environment` | Clean contextual workplace or restrained neutral setting | NORMAL |
| `composition.framing` | Profile/headshot-friendly crop with usable negative space when relevant | NORMAL |
| `lighting` | Soft directional key with clear eyes and natural separation | NORMAL |
| `color.palette` | Trustworthy, controlled, compatible with stated brand colors | SUBTLE |
| `texture.skin` | Polished but recognizable and age-honest | NORMAL |

# Director Guidance

Infer formality only from the stated role, audience, and use. A founder, architect, chef, researcher, and artist require different visual credibility. Keep expression specific and human; background detail should support role without becoming a staged prop list.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK is primary. Wardrobe ADAPT may be refined for fit while preserving design. Environment INSPIRE can supply professional context without copying other people, logos, or confidential material.

# Protective Check

The portrait must read as the stated role and audience while still resembling a real person. Generic suit, folded arms, handshake props, or forced smile do not satisfy role credibility.

# Must Not Override

Identity, age appearance, required attire, brand colors, workplace privacy constraints, logo/text FORBID, or requested informality.

# Avoid

Default navy-suit advertising, crossed-arm power pose, fake office teams, giant blurred monitors, over-whitened teeth, excessive retouching, or invented logos/text.
