# modern-oriental

# Intent

Contemporary East-Asian / 新中式 portraiture — modern cut, restrained palette, spatial calm. Not costume drama.

# Visual Direction

Modern Chinese silhouette and material language in a current photographic frame. Negative space and quiet hierarchy.

# Director Guidance

Serve garment silhouette and space; avoid ornate prop piles. Contemporary face/hair OK. A functional tea service counts as the scene function; add at most one other traditional decorative cue. Choose among ink art, blossom branch, antique vessel, lacquer, or calligraphy—never cluster several with the tea service into a costume-set shorthand.

When the user says “不要古装”, treat the resulting FORBID as decisive: avoid historical cross-collar closure, floor-length robe proportions, theatrical oversized sleeves, dynasty-coded hair ornaments, and costume layering. Replace them with contemporary tailoring, current materials, practical closures, and modern spatial details rather than merely deleting cues.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK from user; wardrobe may ADAPT from ref while recipe shapes cut/color family. Lighting INSPIRE can merge if not clashing.

# Protective Check

The garment must read as contemporary tailoring rather than historical costume, and the space must stay current. In a tea-room scene, one functional tea service plus at most one additional traditional decorative cue is the visible upper boundary.

# Must Not Override

HARD colors, LOCK identity/hair if user locked, FORBID accessories, user “不要古装”.

# Avoid

仙侠 haze, historical cross-collar robe proportions, fixed national-costume template, heavy gold ornament packs, saturated 国潮 collage, or concentrated traditional-symbol staging.
