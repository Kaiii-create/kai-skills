# gufeng-heroine

# Intent

Beauty-forward adult 古风 / 汉服 heroine portraiture for short cues such as “汉服美女” or “古风女主”. This is the polished general route when the user has not requested a dynasty reconstruction, cold xianxia, red-gold palace spectacle, or makeup close-up.

# Visual Direction

Create a bright, comfortable photographed heroine rather than a costume visitor: distinctive adult East Asian beauty, coherent Hanfu-inspired garment structure, controlled medium-high richness, clear face priority, airy mid/high-value space, and one memorable color/light accent. The frame may feel cinematic or romantic but remains photographic unless fantasy is explicit.

# Director Guidance

Choose one photographed moment: turning at a corridor opening, pausing after one step, or lifting one sleeve edge as a breeze settles. Build the action from weight to shoulders to one active hand to gaze. Keep the face large enough to carry “美女 / 女主” intent. Use fabric sheen, embroidery, ornament highlights, and a deliberate accent color to create finish; do not make every surface ornate.

For the bare cue “生成一个汉服美女”, default to a 3:4 thigh-up or three-quarter portrait, medium-high styling richness, a warm-ivory or pale-jade base with one cinnabar/jewel accent, a light-bearing garden or corridor with depth, and bright soft directional light that creates catchlights, luminous natural skin, open shadows and garment separation. These are SOFT defaults.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK remains untouched. Wardrobe ADAPT may supply the exact garment while this recipe controls hierarchy, pose compatibility, and light. A scene or lighting reference may inspire its assigned domain only.

# Protective Check

The face must lead the frame, both eyes must remain readable, and neckline/sleeve/waist structure must be legible. Without explicit dark intent, the first read must be bright and airy: skin is alive, the upper body separates from a pale or light-bearing background zone, shadows stay open, and no deep wall/timber/sky mass dominates. The result must read as a finished heroine portrait rather than a distant tourist record, generic robe photo, e-commerce costume image, or beauty-filter headshot.

# Must Not Override

Explicit dynasty, garment structure, palette, full-body requirement, identity/hair LOCK, accessory FORBID, setting, aspect, or realism/fantasy direction.

# Avoid

Generic pale robe, tiny face, flat overcast face light, dull all-gray-green palette, gloomy underexposure, dominant dark timber or mountain mass, gray skin, storm mood without request, rental-costume styling, cluttered props, mixed dynasties presented as accurate, giant headpiece without structural support, mannequin hands, plastic skin, cheap studio haze, automatic particles, or game-CG rendering.
