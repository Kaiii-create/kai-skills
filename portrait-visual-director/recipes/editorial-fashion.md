# editorial-fashion

# Intent

Fashion-editorial portraiture where garment architecture, attitude, and graphic frame design lead without losing a believable adult person.

# Visual Direction

Make one clear styling proposition. Pose, camera, negative space, and light reveal the clothing silhouette and editorial concept rather than simulating a product catalog.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `subject.pose` | Deliberate graphic line with plausible balance and controlled hands | NORMAL |
| `subject.expression` | Self-possessed, specific, not generic glamour | SUBTLE |
| `wardrobe.outfit` | Preserve construction, volume, material, and styling hierarchy | NORMAL |
| `composition.framing` | Designed crop and asymmetric or geometric placement | NORMAL |
| `composition.hierarchy` | Face and garment silhouette share a clear hierarchy | NORMAL |
| `camera` | Perspective chosen for garment proportion, not novelty distortion | SUBTLE |
| `lighting` | One concept-led key with clean garment readability | NORMAL |
| `color.palette` | Controlled palette serving the collection or stated color story | NORMAL |
| `texture.skin` | Real skin with editorial polish, not poreless beauty filtering | NORMAL |

# Director Guidance

Choose one editorial device—graphic pose, unusual crop, controlled motion, or bold shadow—not all at once. Audit hems, sleeves, hands, and garment tension against the pose. Keep logos, luxury interiors, and decorative spectacle absent unless requested.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK remains untouched. Wardrobe LOCK/ADAPT leads silhouette and material. Pose ADAPT may be refined for balance without importing the pose image's face or outfit. Lighting INSPIRE may define the editorial device.

# Protective Check

The final frame must communicate a specific garment/attitude proposition. If it could be mistaken for a generic e-commerce catalog or beauty-filter portrait, re-direct composition and light without changing protected facts.

# Must Not Override

HARD garment color/count, identity or wardrobe LOCK, accessory FORBID, required setting, or reference scope fences.

# Avoid

Plastic skin, arbitrary couture additions, impossible body geometry, luxury-signifier piles, random Dutch angles, or five competing editorial tricks.
