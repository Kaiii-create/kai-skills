# fine-art-low-key

# Intent

Low-key fine-art portraiture built from sculptural light, controlled shadow, gesture, and negative space while remaining photographic.

# Visual Direction

Shape the face and body with one dominant directional source. Darkness retains structure; the image does not depend on night scenery or theatrical props.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `subject.pose` | Economical gesture with clear silhouette and body tension | NORMAL |
| `subject.expression` | Quiet, inward, or composed rather than melodramatic | SUBTLE |
| `environment` | Sparse dark or tonal space with minimal contextual cues | NORMAL |
| `composition.framing` | Deliberate negative space and sculptural crop | NORMAL |
| `lighting.source` | One large directional or narrow shaped source | NORMAL |
| `lighting.direction` | Model planes of face/body; controlled shadow transition | NORMAL |
| `color.palette` | Restrained monochrome or narrow chromatic family | NORMAL |
| `color.grade` | Deep but separated blacks with protected skin detail | NORMAL |
| `texture.surface` | Matte fabric, skin, and background with selective highlights | SUBTLE |

# Director Guidance

Choose the visible plane that carries expression and keep it readable. Use black levels to create volume, not disappearance. Props must have structural purpose or be omitted.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK is re-lit without changing likeness. Pose ADAPT may supply gesture. Lighting INSPIRE may lead if it supports a single coherent sculptural source.

# Protective Check

At least one eye region, facial plane, and body/garment edge remain readable. If the person collapses into black or becomes a stage character, repair exposure and restraint.

# Must Not Override

Required bright palette, lighting LOCK, scene requirement, identity, skin-tone fidelity, or accessory forbids.

# Avoid

Crushed facial detail, automatic candlelight, religious symbolism, smoke/fog, baroque props, horror grading, or assuming low-key means underexposed.
