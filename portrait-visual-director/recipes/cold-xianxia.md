# cold-xianxia

# Intent

Bright, cold-white, ethereal adult xianxia / 古偶 heroine portraiture driven by moon-white, pale blue, silver, luminous air, distance, and controlled otherworldliness.

# Visual Direction

Create a poised heroine in a bright airy mid/high-value frame with cool-white luminous separation, refined fantasy garment layers, elegant distance, and a readable photographic face. “Cold” describes hue and temperament, not darkness. Atmosphere supports the figure; it does not replace costume structure or become an effects demonstration.

# Director Guidance

Select one restrained atmospheric device—bright thin mist, pale cloud depth, reflected water light, or soft backlit fabric edge. Keep the event small: she has just turned at a corridor, paused at a terrace, or steadied one sleeve after the wind. Give the face a broad luminous neutral key and warm-natural skin recovery so “cold” does not become blue-gray skin, dead eyes, or a gloomy night scene. If night is explicit, build a luminous night with pale cloud/light stone separation rather than a black sky and mountain wall.

# Compatible References

Identity and garment locks remain primary. Lighting inspiration may define direction but must be normalized so the face remains readable. Pose references adapt to long sleeves and trailing layers.

# Protective Check

The image must read at first glance as bright, airy cool xianxia through palette, garment flow, luminous atmosphere, distance and light—not merely through fog. Face, eye catchlights, collar, hands, sleeve edges and waist structure remain readable; skin stays naturally warm/neutral against the cool environment; the largest deep-value mass remains secondary; whites retain texture.

# Must Not Override

Explicit warm palette, historical realism, no-fantasy requirement, identity/hair/wardrobe locks, required props, action, scene, aspect, or accessory forbids.

# Avoid

Dead blue skin, blank expression, white-on-white clipping, gloomy underexposure, storm clouds, deep navy dominance, black mountain wall, large dark sky, fog swallowing the body, floating particle storms, levitation, five simultaneous magic effects, game armor, plastic CG face, excessive gauze, tangled sleeves, giant moon backdrop, or generic blown-white “仙气”.
