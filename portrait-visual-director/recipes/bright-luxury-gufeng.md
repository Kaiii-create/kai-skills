# bright-luxury-gufeng

# Intent

Bright, opulent adult gufeng / 盛唐-inspired heroine portraiture for red-gold, palace, court, ornate headpiece, ceremonial arrival, and high-impact beauty cues.

# Visual Direction

Deliver a commanding heroine frame with a luminous face, clear red/ivory/gold hierarchy, structured ornate clothing, disciplined jewelry highlights, and palace depth. Richness comes from designed concentration, not filling every surface.

# Director Guidance

Use one arrival moment: stopping after a turn, stepping into corridor light, or settling a draped sleeve. Place the brightest controlled highlight on eyes/upper face, then repeat smaller highlights through crown and embroidery. Keep skin warm and alive. Opulence should occupy selected zones; preserve calm fabric and background areas so the face remains dominant.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK is unchanged. Wardrobe references control exact garments/colors when explicit; the recipe shapes light, hierarchy and compatible posing around them. A jewelry reference must not leak identity or scene.

# Protective Check

At first glance the viewer should read an adult palace heroine, a luminous face, a red/ivory/gold hierarchy and premium material separation. Crown, hands, neckline, sleeves and embroidery must remain structurally intelligible. The result cannot collapse into bridal rental styling or decorative CG.

# Must Not Override

Explicit dynasty accuracy, cool/restrained palette, accessory forbids, identity/wardrobe locks, required minimalism, realism level, scene, action, aspect, or no-makeup request.

# Avoid

Wedding-studio cliché, uniform red wash, yellow metallic plastic, jewelry covering the face, tangled dangling ornaments, mixed costume eras presented as accurate, symmetrical mannequin pose, blown skin, excessive bokeh, particle glitter, fantasy palace matte painting, game-CG face, or cheap cosplay fabric.
