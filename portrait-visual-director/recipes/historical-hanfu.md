# historical-hanfu

# Intent

Reality-grounded adult Hanfu portraiture when the user names a dynasty, garment system, reconstruction, historical plausibility, or clearly asks for non-fantasy traditional dress.

# Visual Direction

Make period coherence visible through silhouette, neckline, sleeve, waist, layering, hair, material, and restrained accessories. It is a premium contemporary photograph of historical dress—not a claim of scholarly reconstruction unless the user provides the exact form and source.

# Director Guidance

If a dynasty is explicit, do not import iconic parts from another dynasty for spectacle. If the user only says “真实汉服 / 考据感” without a dynasty, select one internally coherent system and avoid falsely claiming exact reconstruction. Use action that demonstrates dress behavior—descending one step, arranging a sleeve before sitting, or pausing at a doorway—without turning into ceremonial posing.

Keep beauty direction active through face scale, catchlight, grooming, and posture. Unless darkness is explicit, use a bright clear contemporary-photography exposure with pale architectural or garden bounce. Historical restraint does not mean flat light, gray skin, weak contrast, underexposure, or an anonymous costume record.

# Compatible References

Wardrobe LOCK defines the actual garment and outranks inferred period defaults. Identity LOCK remains likeness only. Environment and pose references adapt only within period/garment feasibility.

# Protective Check

Named period and garment clues must agree at the visible structural level. Collar, sleeves, waist, layers and hair must be readable. The subject remains the portrait lead; accuracy may not collapse the frame into catalog documentation.

# Must Not Override

Exact garment/reference locks, user-stated dynasty, requested modern styling, required fantasy direction, identity, color, accessories, scene, aspect, or full-length garment display.

# Avoid

Unlabeled dynasty mixing, generic bathrobe silhouette, fantasy armor, translucent synthetic costume fabric, oversized bridal crown by default, random Japanese/Korean dress elements, studio backdrop scrolls, museum mannequin stiffness, flat documentary light, plastic smoothing, text, logos, or unsupported claims of exact reconstruction.
