# high-key-minimal

# Intent

High-key minimal portraiture with luminous tonal separation, few elements, and clean human presence.

# Visual Direction

Use bright values intentionally while preserving skin, white clothing, and background as distinct tonal surfaces.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `environment` | White, warm-white, or very light minimal studio/interior | NORMAL |
| `subject.pose` | Open, simple line with small natural asymmetry | NORMAL |
| `composition.framing` | Generous clean space and exact edge control | NORMAL |
| `composition.hierarchy` | Face first; silhouette separated by subtle tone/color | NORMAL |
| `lighting.source` | Broad soft key with controlled fill | NORMAL |
| `lighting.quality` | Luminous, low shadow density, preserved micro-contrast | NORMAL |
| `color.palette` | White, cream, pale neutral, or requested light palette | NORMAL |
| `texture.skin` | Visible natural skin and fine facial detail | NORMAL |
| `texture.surface` | Minimal but tactile material separation | SUBTLE |

# Director Guidance

Separate whites by tone, texture, or hue rather than hard outlines. Keep only props that clarify action. Clean does not mean centered, expressionless, or clinical.

# Compatible References

Identity and wardrobe references stay authoritative. A white garment reference may be adapted through tonal separation. Lighting references may INSPIRE only if they preserve the high-key objective.

# Protective Check

Skin highlights, hair edge, white clothing, and background must remain separately readable; clipping them into one white field fails.

# Must Not Override

HARD dark palette, background color, identity, garment tone, action, or composition LOCK.

# Avoid

Blown highlights, gray dirty whites, passport-photo stiffness, medical lighting, poreless skin, empty composition, or unnecessary pastel props.
