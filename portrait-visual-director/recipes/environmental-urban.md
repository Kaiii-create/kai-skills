# environmental-urban

# Intent

Environmental urban portraiture in which the person and the city explain each other through scale, geometry, movement, and depth.

# Visual Direction

Keep the subject unmistakable while allowing architecture, transit, street rhythm, or neighborhood texture to carry meaningful visual weight.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `environment` | Specific contemporary urban fabric with readable spatial layers | NORMAL |
| `moment` | Plausible pause, crossing, arrival, wait, or interaction with place | NORMAL |
| `subject.pose` | Body relates to curb, wall, railing, doorway, or movement path | NORMAL |
| `composition.framing` | Environmental context retained; avoid portrait-mode background erasure | NORMAL |
| `composition.hierarchy` | Face first or co-primary, then architectural line and city depth | NORMAL |
| `camera` | Perspective chosen to express human-to-city scale | NORMAL |
| `lighting` | Motivated available light from actual urban sources | NORMAL |
| `texture.surface` | Concrete, glass, metal, signage shape, or pavement only when scene-relevant | SUBTLE |

# Director Guidance

Choose one spatial relationship and make it legible. Preserve enough depth and context to identify the place type without turning signs, traffic, or crowds into visual noise. Day, dusk, and night are all valid.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK stays primary. Environment or composition references may ADAPT geometry. Pose references control the person only and may not import their street props or background.

# Protective Check

The city must affect composition, scale, or action. A generic bokeh wall behind a close-up does not satisfy this recipe.

# Must Not Override

Named city/place, time, identity, pose LOCK, privacy/safety constraints, or text/logo FORBID.

# Avoid

Automatic neon, tourist-postcard landmarks by default, unreadable crowd clutter, fake signs, extreme background blur, or using architecture merely as wallpaper.
