# analog-film

# Intent

Photographic analog character expressed through restrained color response, grain, highlight behavior, and optical texture—not through decorative damage.

# Visual Direction

Choose one plausible film-language family and keep it consistent across exposure, white balance, saturation, sharpness, and grain.

# Preferred State Patches

| Path | Direction | Strength |
|------|-----------|----------|
| `color.palette` | Restrained photochemical color response suited to scene light | NORMAL |
| `color.grade` | Gentle highlight roll-off, stable shadows, modest saturation | NORMAL |
| `texture.surface` | Fine organic grain scaled to image and exposure | NORMAL |
| `texture.skin` | Natural skin with tonal variation; no digital wax finish | NORMAL |
| `lighting` | Available light or a single plausible flash/daylight balance | NORMAL |
| `camera.depth` | Optical falloff with moderate, believable sharpness | SUBTLE |
| `composition` | Observational crop or deliberate editorial frame | SUBTLE |
| `moment` | Unrehearsed or simply directed beat | SUBTLE |

# Director Guidance

Decide whether the image reads as available-light negative film or restrained direct-flash editorial, based on the request. Grain must be finer in bright areas and more present in shadows; halation belongs only around strong highlights.

# Compatible References

Identity and wardrobe roles remain unchanged. A lighting reference may determine flash versus ambient. Color/texture references may INSPIRE a response but cannot import scene objects.

# Protective Check

Film character must be visible through coherent grain, tonal response, and highlight behavior. Random borders, scratches, date stamps, heavy leaks, or blanket orange tint do not count.

# Must Not Override

HARD color, lighting LOCK, clean-background requirement, identity, or any text/date-stamp FORBID.

# Avoid

Fake dust packs, universal teal-orange, crushed scans, excessive blur, random light leaks, disposable-camera caricature, or period costumes not requested.
