# ancient-beauty-closeup

# Intent

Face-led adult ancient beauty portraiture for 贵女妆、花钿、古风美妆、水光妆、珠玉发饰 and close-up requests where facial character and cosmetic/material detail are the main subject.

# Visual Direction

Build a premium photographic beauty frame: an unmistakably adult East Asian face, specific eye and brow character, controlled luminous makeup, readable hair ornaments, and enough garment neckline to establish ancient styling. Skin remains human and dimensional.

# Director Guidance

Choose one micro-event—a breath after turning, a hair ornament just settled, or eyes meeting the lens. Keep hands out unless one hand can enter cleanly without blocking jaw, jewelry or neckline. Beauty light must retain nose/cheek/jaw modeling; “水光” means selective hydrated highlights, not wet plastic skin.

# Compatible References

Identity LOCK has absolute priority and may not be beautified into another person. Hair or makeup references apply only to their assigned domains. Jewelry references must not import a face, pose or background.

# Protective Check

Both eyes, facial planes, hairline, lips and key ornaments must be structurally clear. Skin must stay dimensional. The ancient identity should be established by grooming, ornament and neckline without filling the frame with props.

# Must Not Override

Identity likeness, no-makeup/low-makeup requests, hair or accessory locks/forbids, exact facial features, requested full-body framing, period accuracy, palette, or aspect.

# Avoid

Teen-coded face, doll proportions, identical influencer face, porcelain-mask smoothing, wet plastic highlights, contact-lens exaggeration, overdrawn lips, false eyelash fan, jewelry touching or entering skin, ornaments obscuring eyes, beauty-ad crop accidents, floating petals, glow fog, text, logos, or illustration rendering unless requested.
