# Wardrobe material logic

- Knit: reveal loop texture, ribbing, weight and fold compression; avoid plastic smoothness.
- Tailoring: reveal shoulder, closure, seam, lapel/neckline and hem tension; pose must not crush structure.
- Silk/satin: use long controlled highlights, not metallic glare.
- Linen/cotton: preserve matte weave, breathable folds and believable thickness.
- Gauze: show translucent-over-opaque layer order; never make the whole garment transparent.
- Brocade/embroidery: concentrate detail in selected zones and preserve quiet fabric areas.
- Leather/metal: keep small specular highlights tied to the motivated light.

# Selection audit

Every selected library item must solve a visible need in this request. Reject a combination that creates two gaze targets, two main events, two primary lights, incompatible material behavior, or a face/garment hierarchy that conflicts with the recipe.
