# Natural Lifestyle Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `natural-lifestyle` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario, fill only `UNSET` fields, and let explicit user or reference constraints win.

## NL01 — Quiet café window
- Match: café, soft daytime, gentle or intellectual temperament.
- Moment: the adult woman has just set down her coffee and turns toward the window, expression relaxed rather than camera-aware.
- Visible design: oatmeal cardigan over a pale inner layer; loose, touchable hair; minimal makeup and visible skin texture.
- Space: a quiet window seat, one ceramic cup, clean wooden tabletop, no visible patrons.
- Frame: vertical waist-up or three-quarter portrait; window creates breathing room toward her gaze.
- Light/finish: broad window light with soft falloff; warm-neutral, true-to-life editorial finish.
- Acceptance anchors: one adult subject only; one cup; readable post-action moment; no posed hand-to-face smile.

## NL02 — Warm home reading nook
- Match: private domestic calm, reading, late-morning ease.
- Moment: she pauses after turning a page and listens toward an off-frame sound, one finger still marking the page.
- Visible design: washed cotton shirt and relaxed trousers; lightly gathered hair with natural flyaways.
- Space: window-side chair, one open book, a single linen cushion, uncluttered room.
- Frame: seated three-quarter portrait with the book low in frame and negative space toward the sound.
- Light/finish: indirect daylight bounced from a pale wall; low contrast and restrained filmic warmth.
- Acceptance anchors: one book only; hands make physical sense; no decorative breakfast spread; no showroom styling.

## NL03 — Morning apartment kitchen
- Match: everyday morning ritual, clean apartment, candid energy.
- Moment: she has just poured water and looks toward the balcony while steam thins above one mug.
- Visible design: soft gray T-shirt under an ivory overshirt; slightly untidy low bun; fresh, unretouched skin.
- Space: compact modern kitchen, one mug and one kettle, clear counters, no other people.
- Frame: medium portrait from across the counter; foreground edge adds depth without blocking the subject.
- Light/finish: cool morning window light plus a faint warm practical; documentary-natural color.
- Acceptance anchors: single subject; no food cluster; action continuity between kettle, mug, and gaze.

### Selection boundary

Keep the scene ordinary, private, and lightly observed. Do not turn these scenarios into beauty ads, influencer selfies, busy cafés, or heavily art-directed interiors.
