# Professional Portrait Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `professional-portrait` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; express competence through context and action, not generic corporate posing.

## PP01 — Architect in the model studio
- Match: design professional, focused confidence, contemporary workplace.
- Moment: the adult architect has just placed one scale model piece and looks toward a colleague outside frame.
- Visible design: dark tailored jacket over a cream knit, practical watch, neat natural hair.
- Space: clean studio table, one architectural model, rolled plans kept secondary, no other people.
- Frame: standing three-quarter portrait with model in the lower foreground and clear facial hierarchy.
- Light/finish: large north-window key plus neutral fill; precise, honest editorial color.
- Acceptance anchors: one person; no readable client documents; model geometry is coherent; no crossed-arm stock pose.

## PP02 — Chef before opening
- Match: culinary professional, calm authority, crafted environment.
- Moment: the chef finishes tying one apron strap and surveys the empty dining room.
- Visible design: crisp off-white chef jacket and charcoal apron; restrained grooming, no theatrical hat.
- Space: pass counter, one folded towel, softly lit empty restaurant, no staff or diners.
- Frame: waist-up environmental portrait with clean counter line and visible hand action.
- Light/finish: warm practical depth with a soft cool key from the kitchen side; natural skin and material texture.
- Acceptance anchors: uniform is credible; hands and apron tie make sense; no food display, flames, or celebrity-chef swagger.

## PP03 — Researcher at a light table
- Match: science or creative research, thoughtful intelligence, modern institution.
- Moment: she slides one transparent sample aside and raises her gaze to reconsider the result.
- Visible design: pale shirt under a slate work coat; simple hair tied low; no costume lab clichés.
- Space: one illuminated work surface, abstract sample shapes, blurred shelving, no readable labels.
- Frame: seated or standing medium portrait with face dominant over the equipment.
- Light/finish: cool task-light underglow balanced by soft side key; clean documentary finish.
- Acceptance anchors: no hazardous procedure; equipment remains plausible but secondary; no text hallucinations or extra colleagues.

### Selection boundary

Professional identity should be visible through one credible action, one environment cue, and composed presence—not logos, fake text, excessive equipment, or stock-photo gestures.
