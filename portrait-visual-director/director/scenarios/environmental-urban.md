# Environmental Urban Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `environmental-urban` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; the city must explain the portrait without stealing subject hierarchy.

## EU01 — Blue-hour elevated walkway
- Match: metropolitan transition, confident adult, layered skyline.
- Moment: she stops at the railing after climbing the final step and looks across traffic flow.
- Visible design: navy coat over a pale gray knit, slim shoulder bag, wind-shifted hair.
- Space: empty foreground walkway, distant towers and traffic below, no nearby pedestrians.
- Frame: vertical three-quarter portrait; railing leads toward the city while the face stays on a clean field.
- Light/finish: blue ambient sky with warm road-light accents; realistic mixed-light balance.
- Acceptance anchors: only one person; no skyline landmark pasted behind her; railing-hand contact is correct.

## EU02 — Brutalist museum plaza
- Match: midday architecture, cultured minimalism, strong scale.
- Moment: she crosses a shadow boundary and briefly turns toward the building entrance.
- Visible design: sand-colored tailored vest, white shirt, charcoal wide trousers.
- Space: monumental concrete planes, one deep opening, plaza cleared of crowds and signage.
- Frame: full-length portrait with architecture occupying meaningful negative space; verticals corrected.
- Light/finish: hard sun shaped by building shadow, soft reflected fill on face, neutral editorial color.
- Acceptance anchors: person remains large enough to read; no tiny tourist figure; no warped façade or random street clutter.

## EU03 — Dawn neighborhood street
- Match: lived-in city, quiet documentary mood, early morning.
- Moment: she adjusts one cuff after buying a folded newspaper, then glances down the street.
- Visible design: olive field jacket, cream tee, straight jeans; natural hair and minimal makeup.
- Space: old storefront shutters, one bicycle, damp pavement, no readable brand text or passersby.
- Frame: knee-up environmental portrait at eye level; street recedes behind one shoulder.
- Light/finish: cool dawn base with a single warm shop light; restrained grain and realistic contrast.
- Acceptance anchors: one bicycle and one folded paper only; background supports era-neutral urban life; no staged street-fashion strut.

### Selection boundary

Show a reciprocal relationship between subject and city. Avoid anonymous wallpaper skylines, crowds, landmark tourism, and exaggerated wide-angle distortion.
