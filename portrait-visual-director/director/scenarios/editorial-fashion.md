# Editorial Fashion Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `editorial-fashion` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; prioritize silhouette, material, and graphic composition without turning the subject into a mannequin.

## EF01 — Silver-gray long coat
- Match: cool minimal fashion story, strong outerwear.
- Moment: the adult model completes a half-turn and the coat hem settles behind her.
- Visible design: long silver-gray wool coat over a tonal fine-knit column; clean hair tucked behind one ear.
- Space: warm-gray seamless studio with no props.
- Frame: vertical full-length composition with deliberate floor margin and asymmetric negative space.
- Light/finish: large side source with narrow edge separation; crisp fabric texture and restrained desaturation.
- Acceptance anchors: complete garment silhouette; feet and hem intact; no handbag, jewelry stack, or wind-machine excess.

## EF02 — Sculptural black dress
- Match: architectural fashion, decisive posture, dark graphic form.
- Moment: she pauses halfway up a concrete stair, weight committed to the upper step.
- Visible design: matte black asymmetrical dress with one sculpted shoulder; minimal metal earring.
- Space: raw concrete stair and one shadow plane, empty of bystanders.
- Frame: low but not distorted three-quarter-length view; stair diagonals support the body line.
- Light/finish: hard skylight softened at the face, deep sculptural shadows, magazine-grade neutral finish.
- Acceptance anchors: anatomy and stair contact are correct; dress remains black with readable detail; no theatrical smoke.

## EF03 — Ivory and vermilion panel
- Match: bold lookbook, graphic color blocking, refined restraint.
- Moment: she steps from behind a vermilion panel and briefly meets the lens.
- Visible design: ivory tailored suit with a sharp waist and a single black inner layer.
- Space: pale studio with one freestanding red panel and no additional set pieces.
- Frame: vertical knee-up portrait; panel occupies no more than one third of frame.
- Light/finish: clean frontal soft light plus subtle side falloff; saturated red controlled away from skin.
- Acceptance anchors: face remains primary; panel does not bisect limbs; no logo, text, or accessory clutter.

### Selection boundary

Editorial means intentional silhouette and composition, not automatic high glamour. Keep one visual thesis per frame.
