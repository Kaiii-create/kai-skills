# Ancient Beauty Close-up Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `ancient-beauty-closeup` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; protect facial hierarchy, historical grooming, and natural skin detail.

## AC01 — Pearl water-glow close-up
- Match: ivory and pearl, quiet historical elegance, soft luminosity.
- Moment: the adult woman raises her gaze after adjusting one pearl drop near her ear.
- Visible design: ivory collar edge, compact period updo, two pearl accents, translucent natural makeup with visible skin texture.
- Space: pale garden-water background with one soft pearl-gray reflection band and light foliage depth, no architecture or dark mass competing with the face.
- Frame: head-and-shoulders three-quarter portrait; hand enters cleanly beside the ear and does not cover the jaw.
- Light/finish: bright broad cool daylight with warm skin bounce, open shadows and delicate catchlights; luminous fine photographic texture.
- Acceptance anchors: one face and one hand only; pearls remain small; face stays warm and dimensional against the pale background; no gray skin, porcelain mask, oversized eyes, or fantasy sparkle.

## AC02 — Red-plum forehead detail
- Match: ivory, muted red, poised Tang-inspired beauty close-up.
- Moment: she turns toward an off-frame voice, creating a slight shift in the hanging earring.
- Visible design: ivory-red collar, compact high updo with restrained gold pins, one small red forehead ornament.
- Space: warm palace wall and a distant blurred plum branch, no columns crossing the head.
- Frame: close three-quarter portrait with the forehead detail visible but secondary to the eyes.
- Light/finish: soft warm side light, controlled red saturation, realistic skin and fine hair detail.
- Acceptance anchors: forehead ornament is small and centered; jewelry does not multiply; no modern contour makeup, doll face, or CGI glow.

## AC03 — Celadon and jade profile
- Match: Song-inspired restraint, cool celadon palette, contemplative profile.
- Moment: she pauses before inserting one simple jade hairpin, gaze lowered naturally.
- Visible design: pale celadon collar, low neat bun, one jade pin, near-bare makeup.
- Space: softly lit paper-screen background with one faint bamboo shadow, no readable motif.
- Frame: close profile-to-three-quarter portrait including the hand, bun, and collar edge.
- Light/finish: diffused window light with soft negative fill; low-contrast museum-quality photographic color.
- Acceptance anchors: pin and fingers are anatomically correct; profile remains adult and natural; no ornate crown, heavy blush, or smooth plastic skin.

### Selection boundary

Close-up beauty comes from expression, grooming, skin, and one meaningful detail. Reject childlike facial proportions, over-retouching, excessive ornaments, and cropped or malformed hands.
