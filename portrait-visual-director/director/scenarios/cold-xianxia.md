# Cold Xianxia Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `cold-xianxia` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; maintain human anatomy and photographic material logic while allowing restrained otherworldly atmosphere.

## CX01 — Dawn cloud terrace
- Match: silver-white palette, distant calm, luminous early-morning cloud setting.
- Moment: the adult woman pauses at the terrace edge after turning, one hand holding the outer sleeve against a light wind.
- Visible design: moon-white layered robes with silver-gray embroidery, narrow translucent outer layer, long dark hair half-bound with one silver pin.
- Space: pale stone terrace above a bright cloud layer, faint blue-gray mountains softened in the far distance, no other figures and no large dark sky.
- Frame: vertical three-quarter portrait; terrace line creates depth without placing her on a cliff edge.
- Light/finish: broad cool-white dawn diffusion with gentle neutral face fill, soft silver fabric-edge light, open shadows and low-to-medium contrast; bright airy photographic realism.
- Acceptance anchors: first read is bright and ethereal; face and moon-white layers separate from pale cloud/stone; skin is alive rather than gray; no levitation, energy beams, glowing eyes, giant moon, weapon, particle storm, dominant dark mass, or clipped white fabric.

## CX02 — Frost-blue misty bamboo morning
- Match: frost-blue robes, quiet alertness, luminous bamboo after morning mist.
- Moment: she hears a movement beyond frame and stops with fingertips just leaving a bamboo stalk.
- Visible design: frost-blue cross-collar layers, charcoal sash, simple dark hair with a slim white jade pin.
- Space: sparse light-green bamboo path, pale damp stones, thin backlit mist in the far layer, no lantern cluster or black forest wall.
- Frame: knee-up portrait with bamboo forming controlled vertical rhythm and clean facial background.
- Light/finish: bright overcast sky key filtered through bamboo plus soft frontal fill and a delicate translucent-sleeve rim; cool-white low saturation, open shadows and natural warm-neutral skin.
- Acceptance anchors: finger contact and gaze align; upper body has a pale clean background zone; frame remains bright and breathable; no duplicated bamboo through limbs, gloomy forest, anime styling, blue skin, or textureless whites.

## CX03 — Silver-gray luminous cloud hall
- Match: serene immortal-sect atmosphere, pale architectural calm, cold dignity without gloom.
- Moment: she crosses an empty hall, then looks back as her trailing sleeve comes to rest.
- Visible design: silver-gray robe with white inner collar and minimal geometric woven edge; high half-up hair without crown.
- Space: pale stone hall with open side colonnade, one distant bright doorway, soft cloud light beyond, thin floor haze limited below the knees.
- Frame: full-length vertical portrait with symmetrical architecture slightly offset by her turn.
- Light/finish: broad cool skylight, gentle frontal pale-stone bounce and narrow edge light; bright mid/high tonal distribution with crisp photographic detail and protected highlights.
- Acceptance anchors: full body and hem intact; face, hair, silver-gray robe and pale hall remain separated; no deep charcoal hall, floating stairs, magic sigils, excessive crown, glossy game-render finish, or blown whites.

### Selection boundary

Fantasy is permitted only as luminous atmosphere, not as a substitute for photography. Keep effects subordinate to face, action, fabric, and spatial credibility. Do not select night by default; an explicit night request must still use pale cloud/light-stone separation and open readable shadows.
