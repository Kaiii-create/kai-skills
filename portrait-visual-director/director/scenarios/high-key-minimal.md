Runtime rule: load only after `high-key-minimal` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; retain facial and garment volume while keeping the palette airy.

## HK01 — Cream monochrome pause
- Match: warm high-key portrait, gentle adult presence, tonal dressing.
- Moment: she completes a small turn and settles her gaze just past the camera.
- Visible design: cream knit top and ivory tailored skirt; soft clean hair, natural makeup.
- Space: warm-white studio with no props and a faint floor-to-wall transition.
- Frame: knee-up vertical portrait with balanced negative space and clear arm separation.
- Light/finish: broad frontal-side source, white bounce, luminous but not clipped whites.
- Acceptance anchors: face retains dimension; outfit edges remain distinct; no washed-out skin or bridal styling.

## HK03 — White shirt and translucent planes
- Match: modern editorial high key, crisp structure, mature simplicity.
- Moment: she leans slightly between two translucent planes and straightens one cuff.
- Visible design: oversized white shirt with stone trousers; minimal stud earring.
- Space: pale-gray translucent set planes, no text or objects.
- Frame: waist-up vertical composition with layered planes creating shallow depth.
- Light/finish: soft top-side light, low contrast with controlled shadow edges, clean neutral grade.
- Acceptance anchors: shirt texture remains visible; planes do not intersect the body; no beauty-ad glow or plastic skin.

### Selection boundary

High key is controlled luminous exposure, not blown highlights. Preserve separation, skin tone, texture, and a readable focal hierarchy.
