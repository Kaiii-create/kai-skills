# Historical Hanfu Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `historical-hanfu` is selected. Choose exactly one dynasty-led scenario and keep silhouette, hair, material, and space mutually compatible.

## HH01 — Song garden study
- Match: Song-inspired restraint, scholar-garden quiet, pale mineral palette.
- Moment: the adult woman closes a slim album and turns toward rain beginning beyond the eaves.
- Visible design: pale celadon beizi over an ivory inner layer and soft gray skirt; narrow borders, low bun, one small hairpin.
- Space: garden study threshold, bamboo shadow, one album, no servant or tea spread.
- Frame: seated or standing three-quarter portrait with clear vertical garment lines.
- Light/finish: bright overcast window light, pale-wall and gentle timber bounce, open shadows, low-saturation luminous photographic finish.
- Acceptance anchors: Song-inspired layering stays restrained; skin and celadon/ivory layers remain clear and bright; no gloomy gray cast, Tang chest line, Ming stand collar, fantasy crown, or modern tailoring.

## HH02 — Ming courtyard correspondence
- Match: Ming-inspired structured elegance, quiet afternoon, mature presence.
- Moment: she has just folded a letter and pauses before placing it into a small wooden box.
- Visible design: deep teal ao with a clear upright collar over a muted red mamian-style skirt; compact period updo and minimal gold pin.
- Space: daylight courtyard threshold with pale plaster, restrained brick/timber edges, one writing table and box, no readable calligraphy or dark room mass.
- Frame: knee-up portrait; table enters as a lower anchor without hiding garment structure.
- Light/finish: bright directional afternoon window light, warm pale-wall/wood fill, open shadows, rich but controlled period color.
- Acceptance anchors: collar and skirt logic remain coherent; hands and paper are correct; face stays luminous against the teal garment; no gloomy interior, mixed-dynasty headdress or palace spectacle.

## HH03 — Tang corridor at sunset
- Match: Tang-inspired confidence, red-gold palette, ceremonial architecture without fantasy.
- Moment: she completes a turn beneath the corridor and lightly steadies one wide sleeve as it settles.
- Visible design: vermilion high-waisted skirt with an ivory short upper garment and gold woven trim; full period updo with limited ornaments.
- Space: palace corridor with red columns and distant roofline, no court crowd.
- Frame: vertical three-quarter to full-length portrait, sunset opening behind one shoulder.
- Light/finish: warm low sun as rim with generous pale-stone corridor fill and protected highlights; bright real-location historical photography.
- Acceptance anchors: one adult subject; sleeve action readable; face and ivory/red layers stay clear; no shadow-heavy corridor, exposed modern glamour neckline, immortal glow, CGI palace, or excessive jewelry.

### Selection boundary

Historical specificity is a compatibility system, not a costume label. If dynasty is user-specified, reject cross-dynasty silhouette and ornament leakage.
