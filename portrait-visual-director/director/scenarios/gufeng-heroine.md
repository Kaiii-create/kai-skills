# Gufeng Heroine Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `gufeng-heroine` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; keep the heroine grounded in a photographed place, material, and moment.

## GH01 — Rain-washed Jiangnan corridor
- Match: celadon, ivory, misty rain, quiet resolve.
- Moment: the adult woman steps down from the covered corridor, lifts one skirt edge clear of the wet stone, and listens beyond the moon gate.
- Visible design: celadon long outer robe over ivory skirt, restrained woven borders, low historical updo with two small pearl pins.
- Space: wet pale flagstones, medium-warm timber corridor, light plaster wall, bamboo and one moon gate, no attendants; no large dark timber field behind the head.
- Frame: vertical full-length portrait at eye level; architecture layers behind without centering her in the gate.
- Light/finish: bright post-rain sky diffusion with warm plaster/timber bounce, open shadows, luminous natural skin and soft wet reflections; realistic fabric weight.
- Acceptance anchors: first read is bright and comfortable; single adult subject; face and celadon/ivory layers separate clearly; complete hem and feet; no gloomy underexposure, umbrella, floating sleeves, magic mist, or modern makeup.

## GH02 — Autumn courtyard decision
- Match: ochre, moss green, mature heroic composure.
- Moment: she stops after turning from a stone table, one hand gathering the outer sleeve while her gaze fixes off frame.
- Visible design: muted ochre cross-collar layers with a moss-green belt, period-appropriate hair and one wooden hairpin.
- Space: quiet courtyard, one old tree, scattered leaves, plain gray wall, no weapon display.
- Frame: knee-up to three-quarter portrait with open wall toward her gaze.
- Light/finish: bright late-morning autumn sun filtered by pale leaves, warm edge and generous light-stone fill, open-shadow cinematic photography.
- Acceptance anchors: turn and sleeve gesture are readable; face is luminous and the wall stays light-bearing; no gloomy grade, sword, armor, supernatural wind, or ornate palace clutter.

## GH03 — Dawn at the moon gate
- Match: pale blue-gray, restrained anticipation, garden architecture.
- Moment: she has just crossed the threshold and lightly touches the stone edge before choosing her direction.
- Visible design: blue-gray layered hanfu with an off-white inner collar; clean bun and one silver pin.
- Space: moon gate edge, shallow pond reflection, sparse willow, empty garden.
- Frame: three-quarter portrait with the gate cropped as an architectural arc, not a halo.
- Light/finish: bright soft dawn side light with pale sky fill, mist limited to luminous distant depth, natural skin and textile detail.
- Acceptance anchors: hand contacts stone plausibly; face and blue-gray layers remain brighter than the immediate garden depth; no extra figure, dark mountain wall, fantasy particles or game-CG gloss.

### Selection boundary

Heroine energy comes from intention, gaze, and restrained action. Avoid weapon shorthand, supernatural effects, exaggerated wind, and decorative overload.
