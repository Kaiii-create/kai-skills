# Fine Art Low-Key Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `fine-art-low-key` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario and preserve readable form inside the shadows.

## FL01 — Charcoal side-light study
- Match: introspective head-and-shoulders portrait, painterly restraint.
- Moment: she turns toward a narrow light after lowering her eyes, expression present but private.
- Visible design: matte charcoal high-neck top; hair loosely swept back; no visible jewelry.
- Space: deep warm-black field with a faint tonal gradient, no props.
- Frame: close vertical portrait with one shoulder receding into shadow.
- Light/finish: narrow soft side light, negative fill, luminous eye and cheek plane, delicate paper-like grain.
- Acceptance anchors: both eyes remain anatomically readable; shadow is not crushed; no smoky fantasy or glossy beauty retouch.

## FL02 — Brown canvas and quiet hands
- Match: seated fine-art portrait, earth palette, emotional stillness.
- Moment: she has just folded a letter out of frame and lets both hands rest loosely in her lap.
- Visible design: umber linen dress with a simple square neckline; natural hair texture.
- Space: painted brown canvas background and one dark wooden chair only.
- Frame: seated three-quarter portrait; hands fully visible and separated from the dress.
- Light/finish: high soft window-like key with slow falloff; muted old-master palette without costume imitation.
- Acceptance anchors: hands are correct and calm; chair does not merge with body; no ornate antiques or theatrical sorrow.

## FL03 — Monochrome window stripe
- Match: graphic black-and-white portrait, disciplined contrast.
- Moment: she crosses a narrow band of window light and pauses with gaze outside frame.
- Visible design: black boat-neck top; simple hair with a few loose strands.
- Space: plain dark room, one vertical light stripe, no visible window frame.
- Frame: chest-up vertical crop; stripe shapes face and shoulder without cutting through the eyes.
- Light/finish: monochrome panchromatic response, deep midtones, fine grain, restrained highlights.
- Acceptance anchors: facial planes remain legible; no zebra-like blinds pattern; no glamour eyeliner or silver-screen cosplay.

### Selection boundary

Low-key means selective illumination and tonal discipline. It does not mean underexposed skin, opaque blacks, melodramatic props, or fantasy haze.
