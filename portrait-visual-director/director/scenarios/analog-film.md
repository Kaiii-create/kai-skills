# Analog Film Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `analog-film` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario; emulate photographic behavior rather than adding a generic vintage filter.

## AF01 — Afternoon balcony negative
- Match: warm domestic nostalgia, 35 mm observational portrait.
- Moment: the adult woman leans back from watering one plant and squints gently into reflected sun.
- Visible design: faded blue cotton shirt and off-white trousers; casually pinned hair.
- Space: small balcony, one terracotta pot, textured wall, no decorative plant jungle.
- Frame: waist-up vertical crop with slight off-center spontaneity and natural lens falloff.
- Light/finish: late-afternoon bounce, soft highlight rolloff, moderate color-negative grain and subtle halation.
- Acceptance anchors: grain respects tonal areas; skin is not orange; no fake date stamp, border, dust overlay, or light leak.

## AF02 — Restrained direct flash at night
- Match: candid nightlife without party chaos, compact-camera immediacy.
- Moment: she turns after pushing open a small shop door, caught between steps rather than posing.
- Visible design: black cardigan, white tank, dark denim; slightly tousled hair.
- Space: quiet corner shop exterior, one warm sign shape without readable text, empty sidewalk.
- Frame: knee-up vertical portrait with slight flash flattening but correct proportions.
- Light/finish: low-power direct flash balanced with ambient tungsten; cool shadows, realistic grain, no overexposure.
- Acceptance anchors: eyes and skin remain natural; no red-eye, paparazzi crowd, disposable-camera gimmick, or crushed background.

## AF03 — Train-window color negative
- Match: travel pause, introspective daylight, layered reflection.
- Moment: she rests one hand beside the window after the train leaves a station and watches the landscape shift.
- Visible design: rust knit sweater and neutral skirt; loose shoulder-length hair.
- Space: simple train seat, one window, faint single-layer reflection, no other passengers.
- Frame: seated medium portrait with reflection kept away from eyes and mouth.
- Light/finish: cool window key, warm carriage fill, fine-grain color-negative palette with gentle cyan shadows.
- Acceptance anchors: one person only; reflection is not a duplicate subject; no retro costume or heavy sepia cast.

### Selection boundary

Analog character comes from grain structure, rolloff, color response, and capture behavior—not borders, timestamps, leaks, scratches, or blanket desaturation.
