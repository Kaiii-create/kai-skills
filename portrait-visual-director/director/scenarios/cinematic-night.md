# Cinematic Night Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `cinematic-night` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario and establish a motivated night-light story before adding color accents.

## CN01 — Convenience-store spill
- Match: solitary urban pause, modern casual wardrobe, mixed practical light.
- Moment: the adult woman exits the store, checks the street, and holds the door for a beat.
- Visible design: dark leather jacket over a muted knit top; natural hair moved by light wind.
- Space: storefront threshold, one lit refrigerator band inside, wet curb outside, no visible customers.
- Frame: medium three-quarter portrait from street level; doorway frames the body without crowding.
- Light/finish: cool interior spill as key, warm street practical as rim; cinematic but believable color separation.
- Acceptance anchors: one person; light sources visible or inferable; no neon overload, cyberpunk effects, or floating particles.

## CN02 — Rainy bus-stop interval
- Match: reflective mood, rain, blue-hour-to-night transition.
- Moment: she lowers a clear umbrella after seeing the approaching bus light beyond frame.
- Visible design: slate trench coat, fine knit, damp loose strands, understated makeup.
- Space: glass bus shelter, a single route-light panel without readable text, rain-dark pavement.
- Frame: vertical knee-up portrait with reflections kept secondary to her face.
- Light/finish: soft shelter light across the face, distant red taillight accents, controlled wet reflections.
- Acceptance anchors: umbrella geometry and hands are correct; shelter remains empty; no dramatic storm or fashion-campaign posing.

## CN03 — Hotel window after dark
- Match: quiet metropolitan luxury, mature urban temperament.
- Moment: she has drawn back the curtain and watches the city while one hand still holds the fabric.
- Visible design: black satin blouse and tailored trousers; softly brushed shoulder-length hair.
- Space: dark hotel corner, one warm lamp, layered city lights through glass, no extra furniture clutter.
- Frame: waist-up or three-quarter profile; city becomes depth and context rather than the subject.
- Light/finish: warm lamp edge plus cool window fill; deep blacks with preserved skin tone and highlight detail.
- Acceptance anchors: no reflected duplicate person; city bokeh does not cover the face; no glamour-gown cliché.

### Selection boundary

Night atmosphere must come from motivated practical sources and spatial depth. Reject arbitrary neon, fantasy glow, or crushed facial detail.
