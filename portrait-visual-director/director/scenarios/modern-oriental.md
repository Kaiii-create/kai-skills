## MO01 — Restrained modern tea room
- Match: black, ivory, and a small vermilion accent; quiet luxury.
- Moment: the adult woman has just aligned a teacup and pauses with her gaze beyond the table.
- Visible design: black wrap jacket with an ivory architectural collar and one slim red cuff detail; modern fabric, no robe construction.
- Space: contemporary tea room with dark timber planes, one minimal tea service, one abstract red panel.
- Frame: seated three-quarter portrait with generous wall space; subject offset from center.
- Light/finish: directional window light softened by wood reflections; matte editorial contrast.
- Acceptance anchors: modern silhouette; only one restrained cultural cue; no antique prop pile, no hanfu, no fantasy styling.

## MO02 — Contemporary gallery passage
- Match: cultured, composed, urban; fashion-forward new Chinese design.
- Moment: she slows between gallery rooms and turns her shoulder back toward a distant work.
- Visible design: ivory structured top with asymmetric black trousers and a narrow lacquer-red belt.
- Space: pale stone gallery, one ink-like abstract installation, uninterrupted negative space.
- Frame: vertical full or three-quarter length; strong architectural lines and a clean silhouette.
- Light/finish: skylight with soft floor bounce; neutral whites, deep controlled blacks, restrained red.
- Acceptance anchors: contemporary setting; no museum crowd; no calligraphy text; pose must feel mid-transition rather than ceremonial.

## MO03 — Slatted-light residence
- Match: intimate modern residence, mature elegance, graphic shadow.
- Moment: she opens a timber screen and stops as light reaches her face and hand.
- Visible design: charcoal sleeveless long vest over a cream column dress; subtle frog-knot geometry expressed as seam detail only.
- Space: minimalist residence with one screen, one low bench, and no ornamental clutter.
- Frame: knee-up portrait using the screen as a vertical frame; ample calm space behind her.
- Light/finish: late-afternoon slatted light softened across skin; refined architectural photography finish.
- Acceptance anchors: hand contact is plausible; cultural influence remains abstract; no qipao cosplay, lanterns, dragons, or palace décor.

### Selection boundary

The modern half must dominate. Use Eastern proportion, restraint, material, or line—not a collection of traditional symbols.
