# Bright Luxury Gufeng Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `bright-luxury-gufeng` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario and express luxury through material, spacing, and controlled ornament density.

## BL01 — Red-and-gold palace corridor
- Match: vermilion, ivory, antique gold; luminous ceremonial portrait.
- Moment: the adult woman has just turned beneath the eaves and lightly gathers one embroidered sleeve.
- Visible design: ivory inner dress, vermilion outer robe, antique-gold borders, structured historical updo with a compact ornament cluster.
- Space: sunlit palace corridor, red columns, one distant roofline, no attendants.
- Frame: vertical three-quarter portrait with corridor recession and clean separation around the headdress.
- Light/finish: bright late-afternoon backlight balanced by warm open fill from pale stone; radiant natural skin, open shadows and detailed brocade.
- Acceptance anchors: jewelry count stays limited; face dominates ornament; frame is luminous rather than shadow-heavy; no halo, floating petals, palace crowd, dark corridor mass, or plastic CG finish.

## BL02 — Vermilion terrace arrival
- Match: grand arrival, rich color, full-length garment display.
- Moment: she reaches the top of the terrace steps and pauses as the outer skirt settles around her feet.
- Visible design: deep red layered gown with ivory paneling and restrained gold thread; balanced hair ornaments with no oversized crown.
- Space: broad stone terrace, vermilion doors, open sky, no guards or entourage.
- Frame: eye-level full-length portrait with intact train and architectural scale kept secondary.
- Light/finish: clean morning sun from the side, warm stone bounce, polished historical editorial photography.
- Acceptance anchors: feet and hem geometry are correct; no bridal staging, supernatural glow, heavy veil, or modern red-carpet pose.

## BL03 — Ivory-gold hero close portrait
- Match: refined close portrait, luminous materials, restrained red accent.
- Moment: she turns from the window and holds a folded red silk drape low at the edge of frame.
- Visible design: ivory-gold embroidered upper layers, one red shoulder accent, elegant period hair with pearl and gold details kept sparse.
- Space: warm ivory interior with one vermilion lacquer edge and a bright lattice-window plane, no furniture clutter or dark wall mass.
- Frame: chest-up vertical portrait; drape is a corner accent, not a background wall.
- Light/finish: broad bright window key across both eyes and cheek, warm ivory bounce, luminous mid/high values, high-detail textile and natural-skin finish.
- Acceptance anchors: eyes remain primary; red occupies a minority of frame; upper body stays separated from the light interior; no underexposure, jewelry curtain, cleavage emphasis, or beauty-filter smoothing.

### Selection boundary

Luxury is not maximum decoration. Use one dominant material story, one controlled ornament cluster, and enough empty space for the person to remain the subject.
