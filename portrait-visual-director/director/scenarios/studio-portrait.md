# Studio Portrait Scenario Bank

Runtime rule: load only after `studio-portrait` is selected. Choose exactly one scenario and make expression, pose, background, and light work as one controlled system.

## SP01 — Warm-gray knit portrait
- Match: approachable adult portrait, natural texture, timeless wardrobe.
- Moment: she settles after a breath, gaze just beside the lens, shoulders relaxed.
- Visible design: oatmeal rib-knit top, softly parted hair, near-invisible makeup.
- Space: seamless warm-gray background, no props.
- Frame: chest-up portrait with comfortable headroom and both shoulders clearly shaped.
- Light/finish: large feathered key, white bounce, delicate hair separation; realistic pores and tonal skin variation.
- Acceptance anchors: no beauty-filter smoothing; no forced smile; catchlights and shadow direction agree.

## SP02 — Deep navy and satin
- Match: composed, mature, evening-leaning studio portrait.
- Moment: she turns from profile into a calm three-quarter gaze, chin level.
- Visible design: muted bronze satin blouse against a deep navy background; one small earring.
- Space: seamless navy field with no visible set edge.
- Frame: waist-up vertical portrait; hands lightly enter the lower frame without becoming a pose gimmick.
- Light/finish: narrow soft key across cheek and collarbone, subtle negative fill, preserved satin detail.
- Acceptance anchors: skin stays neutral; blouse highlights are not clipped; hands are complete and relaxed.

## SP03 — Cream cyclorama full length
- Match: clean personal branding, modern full-length portrait.
- Moment: she finishes one step and lets her arms settle naturally at different heights.
- Visible design: cream shirt, tailored stone trousers, simple flats; tidy but touchable hair.
- Space: cream cyclorama, one faint floor shadow, no furniture.
- Frame: full body with intact feet and breathing space above; eye-level lens.
- Light/finish: broad high side light and soft frontal fill; clean, low-saturation commercial finish.
- Acceptance anchors: no floating feet; trouser lines remain natural; pose is asymmetrical but stable.

### Selection boundary

Studio control should reveal the person, not erase texture or manufacture a rigid catalog pose.
