# Batch Retouch Workflow

For multiple event/wedding/portrait images, build a consistent style profile while respecting per-image content.

When feasible, establish a small representative sample before broad application. Deterministic tone/color/crop/cleanup can be consistently batched. Structural generative edits should be scoped and QC'd per image; do not blindly propagate face/body reshaping across different subjects.

Keep identity and person-specific instructions isolated. Preserve a stable master and consistent export settings.
