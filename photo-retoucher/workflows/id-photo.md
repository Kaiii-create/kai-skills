# ID Photo Workflow

Distinguish **ID-style portrait** from **official submission photo**.

General ID-style: center subject, clean hair edges, requested solid background (white/blue/red/custom), restrained retouch, optional professional clothing, target crop/print sheet.

Official submission: apply `policies/official-id-photo.md`; verify jurisdiction/document requirements from official sources when possible. Compliance first; do not infer generic one-inch/two-inch rules when a specific authority is named.

Solid backgrounds: prefer deterministic subject mask + exact color fill. Wardrobe synthesis must preserve neck/shoulder anatomy and face identity. Do not claim a generated suit is acceptable for an authority unless verified.
