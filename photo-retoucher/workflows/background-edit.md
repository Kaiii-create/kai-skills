# Background & Scene Workflow

Covers replacement, cleanup, object/person removal, expansion and depth blur.

For background-only requests: subject strict-lock. Preserve hair, veil, translucent fabric and fine edges. Secondary changes are limited to integration: ambient color, rim light, contact shadow, depth/grain/sharpness.

Object removal must reconstruct plausible continuous texture/structure. Expansion should preserve subject and composition; prefer expansion over cropping important body/head regions when changing aspect ratio.
