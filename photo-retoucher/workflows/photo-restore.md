# Photo Restoration Workflow

Assess scratches, dust, stains, fading, yellowing, contrast, tears/missing regions, compression, noise and blur.

Prefer faithful cleanup: tone/color restoration → dust/scratch healing → localized reconstruction → optional colorization → output enhancement.

Do not claim synthesized faces/textures in missing or severely blurred regions are historically accurate recovered details. If colorizing, describe it as inferred colorization.
